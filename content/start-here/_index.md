This site is the IT team's source of truth for how Mountaincraft Makerspace's technology is set up and run.

## In scope

- Services the IT team runs or administers (Wi-Fi, accounts, door access, printers, Slack, this site).
- The network: ISP, hardware, topology, VLANs.
- Runbooks for routine tasks and outages.
- IT policies: acceptable use, access, data handling.
- Decisions the team has made and why.

## Out of scope

- Org-wide topics (membership, tool sign-offs, events). These may get their own site later. See `PLAN.md` in the repo.
- Secrets. Passwords, keys and codes live in the password manager. Pages name the item, never the value.

## Who can change it

Anyone can request a change. IT team members review and merge. See [Contributing](/start-here/contributing/).
